Who we are

jwmarcus.com is the professional website of John Marcus III and is operated by Fractional Ventures LLC. This notice explains what information is collected when you visit the site and how it is used.

Visiting the site

This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and sets no cookies of its own. It does not run advertising trackers or analytics that identify you.

Some parts of the page are delivered by other companies. When your browser loads them, it sends those companies a standard request that includes your IP address and browser details. Each handles that information under its own privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and records standard technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the page.
  • Wistia hosts the background video on the home page, which plays on larger screens.
  • Google Maps draws the map near the bottom of the home page.

Links to other sites

This site links to LinkedIn and to John's blog. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which apply once you follow a link.

When you get in touch

If you contact John, for example through LinkedIn, he keeps what you send, including your name and contact details, so he can reply and follow up about your request. This information is not sold, and it is shared only when needed to respond to you or when the law requires it.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, except where the law requires it to be kept. Send your request through the contact option below and you will receive a response within a reasonable time.

Changes to this notice
