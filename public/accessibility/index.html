Accessibility

Last reviewed September 24, 2026

Our commitment

Fractional Ventures LLC is committed to making jwmarcus.com accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities.

Our goal is for this website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. WCAG is an international technical standard for making web content more accessible.

What we have done

  • A "Skip to content" link and labeled page regions for keyboard and screen reader users.
  • A clearly visible focus indicator on every link and button.
  • A logical heading structure and descriptive names for links and controls.
  • A button to pause the background video on the home page. If your device is set to reduce motion, the video starts paused and page scrolling is not animated.
  • Layouts that reflow for phones, tablets, and desktop screens and remain usable when zoomed.
  • Text and background colors that meet WCAG contrast requirements.

How we review the site

We use automated accessibility testing and manual review. Our checks include keyboard navigation, color contrast, heading structure, text alternatives for images, readable zoom and reflow, clear link and control names, and reduced-motion preferences.

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Automated tools cannot identify every barrier, so we also review the site by hand and respond to problems reported by visitors.

Known limitations

The background video and map are provided by Wistia and Google. They are decorative, are hidden from screen readers, and do not carry any information that is not also on the page.

Need help or found a barrier?

If you have difficulty using any part of this site or need its content in another format, please get in touch. Tell us which page you were using and what problem you encountered, and we will work to provide the information and address the barrier.
